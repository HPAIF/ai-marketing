# Obligations, deadlines & recommended actions

> The founder's marketing to-do list. Rows with status `open` and a due date within 30 days are
> shown at the start of every session by the plugin's session hook, so keep the table format
> exactly: `| ID | YYYY-MM-DD | type | what | source | open/done/dropped |`.
> type: deadline (fixed date, e.g. a launch or event) · recurring (e.g. monthly review) ·
> action (recommended) · approval (a draft or decision waiting for the founder) · watch.
> Use `—` as the due date when there is none. Never delete rows; mark them done/dropped.
